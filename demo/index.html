One road segment. One ID. Every dataset.

Real, current 511 events published by four state DOTs — Iowa, Nebraska, Minnesota, and Missouri — conflated once to Overture GERS IDs across the I-80 / I-35 / I-29 / I-70 / I-94 freight network. 99% of interstate point events auto-matched, median snap distance 3.4 meters; I-35 events read as one keyed spine through Missouri, Iowa, and Minnesota. A container leaving the Port of Long Beach crosses a dozen jurisdictions' data systems before the heartland — joined on GERS, its route reads as one dataset, so a dispatcher can plan around construction before the trip the way airlines plan around weather. Each state keeps its own schema and linear referencing (Iowa's RAMS route IDs come through untouched); the GERS ID is only the join key. Click any segment or event.

Why an ID beats a geometry handshake

Today every producer ships lat/lon or its own linear reference, and every consumer re-conflates against its own basemap — per feed, per vendor, per state. A shared GERS reference makes the join a key lookup. The conflation shown here ran once, and its provenance (method, snap distance, route agreement) travels with each match.

Freight plans around it, like aviation does

Airlines plan every departure around weather and NOTAMs; trucking has no equivalent for the road network. A container out of the Port of Long Beach crosses a dozen jurisdictions before the Midwest — with four states' events on one keyed network, a dispatcher can route pre-trip around construction the way an airline routes around a storm. Add authoritative truck-route and restriction data on the same IDs and the return channel exists too — and once states subscribe to each other's event layers, a storm on one corridor can automatically trigger construction holds and debris surges on the detour routes, the way aviation flow management holds and reroutes around weather.

A return path for vehicle fleets

AV and ADAS fleets already detect cones, lane shifts, workers, hard-braking clusters, and lane-level volumes. Referenced to GERS (as a WZDx/CWZ location method or an SAE J3224 SDSM profile), those observations confirm state feeds — and surface what the state feed missed.
